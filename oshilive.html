GAME SHOWCASE

Discover HololiveDeckConstructor

Explore core features of the executable PC client designed for learning, building, and battling.

1. DECK BUILDING & SHARING

Deck Creation & 1-Click Code Sharing

Choose your favorite Oshi (La+ Darknesss, Sora, Suisei, Marine...), explore the full card database, and create 50-main + 20-cheer decks. Generate your HDC1- code in 1 click to share instantly with friends.

2. REAL-TIME DUELS

Matches VS Rival AI & Online Multiplayer

Compete online against friends in real-time or practice solo against the Rival AI. Includes initial hand Redraw (Mulligan) system, Cheer Deck management, Stage zones, and turn timers.

3. OFFICIAL RULES & LEARNING

Official Game Engine & Ideal for Beginners

Strictly follows the official hololive Official Card Game rulebook. Perfect for learning step-by-step: turn phases, stage placement, Bloom evolutions, Cheer assignment, and HP calculation.

4. PROFILE CUSTOMIZATION

Full Player Profile Customization

Customize your player profile to your style: choose your favorite Hololive Oshi, profile picture, ornamental frame, background wallpaper, and view global stats.
